# Premium Quality Checklist

Validation criteria for landing page projects. Use after compiling the PRD to verify all quality areas are addressed. If an area is missing, flag it to the developer before finalizing.

---

## 1. First Impression (50ms Test)

Users form a lasting quality judgment within ~50 milliseconds. This judgment persists even when later evidence contradicts it.

- [ ] **Clear purpose on arrival** — Visitors immediately understand what you do, who it's for, and why it matters without scrolling
- [ ] **No template energy** — Nothing looks templated, rushed, or thrown together. Even simple layouts feel deliberate and intentional
- [ ] **Immediate trust signals** — Professional tone, confident messaging, and visual clarity prevent the "this feels sketchy" reaction
- [ ] **Visual hierarchy is instant** — One dominant element, clear reading path, no competing focal points
- [ ] **Space is generous** — Crowded layouts signal low quality. When in doubt, add more space

---

## 2. Visual Assets

Cheap sites are text-heavy. Premium sites have visual structure.

- [ ] **Custom or brand-aligned graphics** — Visual elements are designed for the brand, not pulled from generic libraries
- [ ] **Graphics serve a purpose** — They guide attention, break up content, and make information easier to digest
- [ ] **No visual noise** — Nothing looks decorative "just because". Every asset earns its place
- [ ] **Photography feels authentic** — No generic stock photos that could belong to any company
- [ ] **Consistent visual language** — Every graphic matches the tone, color palette, and personality of the brand

---

## 3. Brand Foundations

Premium sites are built on strong brand fundamentals.

### Logo
- [ ] **Intentional, professional logo** — Simple is fine, amateur is not. The logo looks considered and credible

### Color Palette
- [ ] **Cohesive color system** — Colors work together to create a consistent mood, not visual chaos
- [ ] **Restraint over variety** — 2-3 main colors used with confidence. Each color has a specific job (brand, CTA, accent)
- [ ] **Contrast meets accessibility** — 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for interactive elements (WCAG AA)

### Typography
- [ ] **Sophisticated font choices** — Fonts elevate the brand rather than cheapen it
- [ ] **Readability comes first** — No outdated, decorative, or hard-to-read fonts in body text
- [ ] **Limited type scale** — Maximum 6 sizes across the entire page

---

## 4. Motion & Interaction

Premium websites feel alive, not chaotic.

- [ ] **Scroll-based motion** — Elements fade or move naturally as users scroll
- [ ] **Interactive feedback** — Buttons and links respond when hovered or clicked (100-150ms)
- [ ] **No distracting gimmicks** — Nothing spins, bounces, or steals attention from the content
- [ ] **Reduced friction** — Animations guide users instead of confusing them
- [ ] **Respects preferences** — Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` with instant transitions as fallback
- [ ] **Loading feels fast** — Skeleton screens over spinners. Optimistic rendering where possible

---

## 5. Structure & Conversion

This is where many "nice-looking" sites fail.

- [ ] **Logical sitemap** — Sections make sense together, not just individually
- [ ] **Clear user journey** — Each section leads naturally to the next step
- [ ] **No decision paralysis** — Visitors always know where to go next. One primary CTA per viewport
- [ ] **Strong, visible CTAs** — Calls to action are obvious, easy to find, and easy to take
- [ ] **Consistent CTA** — The same primary action is repeated throughout the page
- [ ] **SEO-ready structure** — Proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3), internal linking, meta tags, and structured data
- [ ] **Mobile-first** — Layout works in single column before expanding. Touch targets 44px minimum

---

## 6. Client Autonomy

A premium website empowers its owner.

- [ ] **Content is self-manageable** — Owner can update text, images, and blog posts without developer dependency
- [ ] **No black-box build** — The site is not fragile, locked down, or intimidating to edit
- [ ] **Built for longevity** — Adding pages, updating services, and refreshing content is straightforward
- [ ] **Platform fits the owner** — CMS complexity matches the owner's technical ability

---

## 7. Handover & Support

Premium is not just the build — it's the experience.

- [ ] **Structured handover training** — Owner is shown exactly how to use and manage the site
- [ ] **Clear expectations set** — Owner knows what they can do themselves and when to ask for help
- [ ] **Post-launch support window** — Questions and issues are handled without friction after launch
- [ ] **Monitoring configured** — Analytics, error tracking, and performance monitoring are active from day one
- [ ] **Iteration plan exists** — There is a defined process for reviewing performance and making improvements

---

## How to Use This Checklist

After compiling the PRD, go through each area:

1. If the area is covered in the PRD, check the relevant items
2. If an area is missing entirely, ask the developer: "The PRD doesn't address [area]. Should we add a section for this or is it out of scope?"
3. For audit mode, map observed issues to specific checklist items to create prioritized recommendations
