# Interview Questions

Questions organized in 5 thematic blocks. Each block has mandatory questions and challenge prompts for vague or incomplete answers.

Ask one question at a time. Use challenge prompts when responses lack specificity.

---

## Block 1: Strategy & Goals

The foundation. Every subsequent decision flows from these answers.

**Questions:**

1. **Target audience:** Who exactly lands on this page? What do they already know about the problem or the solution before arriving?
2. **The One Thing:** In one sentence, what is the primary action we want the user to take? (e.g., "Schedule a consultation", "Start a free trial")
3. **Success definition:** What metrics or outcomes will define this project as successful in 3 months?
4. **Traffic source:** How do users arrive at this page? (organic search, paid ads, social media, referral, email campaigns)
5. **User segments:** Are there different profiles (B2B vs B2C, cold vs warm traffic)? How do we show them relevant content?

**Challenge prompts:**
- "If you say 'increase sales' — by how much? Compared to what baseline?"
- "'Everyone' is not a target audience. Describe one specific person who would land here."
- "What happens if the user does NOT take the primary action? Is there a secondary path?"
- "You mentioned multiple goals. Which ONE matters most if you had to pick?"

---

## Block 2: Structure & Hierarchy

Defining the map before drawing the territory.

**Questions:**

1. **Sections:** What sections does the page need? List them in the order you imagine them.
2. **Message hierarchy:**
   - Primary: What is the first thing the eye must catch?
   - Secondary: What information supports the main proposition?
   - Tertiary: Technical details, FAQs, or social proof?
3. **Content status:** Does the copy already exist or does it need to be written? Who writes it?
4. **Social proof:** What trust elements are available? (testimonials, client logos, case studies, metrics, certifications)
5. **Above the fold:** What must be visible without scrolling?

**Challenge prompts:**
- "You listed 8 sections — which 3 are essential if we had to ship tomorrow?"
- "That's a lot of text for the hero. Can you say it in 10 words or fewer?"
- "Where does social proof appear? If it's at the bottom, most visitors will never see it."
- "Is this section for the user's benefit or for the business's ego?"

---

## Block 3: Design Direction

Aligning aesthetics with function and brand identity.

**Questions:**

1. **Brand assets:** Does a logo, color palette, and typography system exist? What's their quality?
2. **Visual references:** Share 2-3 sites or styles that represent the desired feel. What specifically do you like about each?
3. **Anti-references:** What should the landing NOT look like? What styles to avoid?
4. **Photography/graphics:** Custom assets or stock? Is there budget for custom work?
5. **Existing brand guidelines:** Is there a brand guide or design system to follow?

**Challenge prompts:**
- "'Modern and clean' — everyone says that. What specifically makes a site feel right to you?"
- "You referenced 3 sites with completely different aesthetics. Which one is closest?"
- "Stock photography often kills trust. How will you make imagery feel authentic?"
- "If the logo is weak, the entire page will feel amateur. Is improving it in scope?"

---

## Block 4: Technical & Performance

Preparing for the reality of maintenance and growth.

**Questions:**

1. **Platform/CMS:** What platform will host this? Does the site owner need to edit content without developer help?
2. **SEO requirements:** Target keywords? Are heading structure (H1, H2) and page speed priorities?
3. **Analytics:** What measurement tools will be configured? What events need tracking? (form submissions, scroll depth, CTA clicks)
4. **Contact channels:** What ways can users reach out? (form, direct email, phone, WhatsApp, live chat)
5. **Integrations:** Any third-party services? (CRM, email marketing, payment, booking)
6. **Performance targets:** Page load time goal? Core Web Vitals targets?

**Challenge prompts:**
- "Who will update the content after launch? If nobody, it'll go stale in 3 months."
- "You want a contact form AND email AND WhatsApp AND phone — that's 4 friction points. Which one converts best for your audience?"
- "No analytics means no way to know what's working. What's the minimum you'll track?"
- "If the CMS is too complex, the owner won't use it. Simpler is better."

---

## Block 5: Launch & Post-launch

The launch is the beginning, not the end.

**Questions:**

1. **Training:** Who needs to learn to manage the site? What do they need to know?
2. **Support window:** What post-launch support period is included? What does it cover?
3. **Iteration plan:** How will performance be measured after launch? What triggers a design change?
4. **Content calendar:** Is there a plan for ongoing content (blog, case studies, updates)?
5. **Timeline:** When does this need to go live? Are there hard deadlines?

**Challenge prompts:**
- "Launch is day 1, not the finish line. What's the plan for week 2?"
- "If there's no training session, the client will call you for every small change."
- "A 3-month review cycle means 3 months of potentially losing conversions. Can you check sooner?"
- "No content plan after launch means the site becomes a static brochure. Is that acceptable?"
